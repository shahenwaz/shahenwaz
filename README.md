## About Me

I'm **Shahenwaz Muzahid**, a final-year **Forensic Cybersecurity student** in Ireland with a strong interest in Digital Forensics, Incident Response, SOC and Cybersecurity.

Alongside cybersecurity, I enjoy **software development** - building web applications, experimenting with technologies, and applying security principles to the systems I create.

My approach is simple: **learn the fundamentals, experiment in practical environments, and build things that solve real problems.**

## Hands-on Experience

- **Digital Forensics:** Forensic imaging, evidence integrity, metadata examination, and introductory forensic investigations.
- **Security Monitoring:** T-Pot honeypot deployment, log analysis, and investigation of suspicious activity.
- **Application Security:** Vulnerability assessment, threat modelling, OWASP principles, and secure development practices.
- **Network Security:** Traffic analysis with Wireshark, network reconnaissance, and security testing in lab environments.
- **Security Labs:** Practical learning through TryHackMe, university labs, and independent experimentation.
